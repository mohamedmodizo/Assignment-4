DATA VISUALIZATION

I was tasked to analyse data and develop a report about my analysis. This was how the question was;

"This week, you will be analysing data for the Marketing team. GlobalShala has been running ad campaigns on Facebook for an event called Superhero U, and the Marketing team needs your help identifying at least one campaign to recommend discontinuing to cut costs. At the end of the week, you will select one or more ad campaigns to recommend discontinuing based on your team’s analysis."

After analysing and coming up with different visualisations, I have come up with the following report.

SUPERHERO U CAMPAIGN ANALYSIS REPORT

Why This Analysis Was Done

GlobalShala asked us to review Facebook ads for the Superhero U event. We looked for campaigns that cost too much but delivered poor results.

1) What I Looked At
- Amount Spent
- Clicks
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Cost Per Result (CPR)
- Campaign Efficiency

2) Visual Tools Used
- Bar Charts for Spend, CTR, CPR
- Scatter Plot for Spend vs Clicks
- Bubble Chart for CTR vs CPR vs Spend

3) Best Performing Campaigns
SHU_Students (Nigeria): Great results, low cost and
SHU_Students (India): Efficient and engaging

4) I recommend keeping and investing more in these.
Campaign to Discontinue

4.1) SHU_6 (Educators and Principals):
- High spend
- Low engagement (lowest CTR)
- Very expensive (highest CPR)

I recommend discontinuing this campaign.

5) Final Recommendation
Keep campaigns that work well and are cost-effective.
Stop spending on poor-performing campaigns.
This will help the team reach more people while saving money.
